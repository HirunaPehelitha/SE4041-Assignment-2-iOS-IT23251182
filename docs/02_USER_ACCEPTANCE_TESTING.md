# 2. User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

The assignment asks for user acceptance testing with real people. **You must run these sessions yourself**
with 3–5 people (classmates, family or friends) and record what they actually said. Do not invent results.

## 2.1 How to run a session (about 10 minutes per person)

1. Install BookNest on your iPhone or iPad (or use the simulator on your Mac).
2. Delete the app first so each tester starts fresh with the 3 sample books.
3. Give the tester the phone and read the tasks below **one at a time**. Don't help unless they are stuck for more than 1 minute.
4. For each task, write down: completed? (Yes / With help / No), time taken, and anything they said.
5. At the end, ask the questionnaire questions.
6. Ask for permission before taking a photo or screenshot of the session for your report.

## 2.2 Tasks

| # | Task given to the tester | Feature tested | Success when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Open *The Elephant Who Loved Rain* and read to page 2." | Library, Reader, swipe | Page shows "Page 2 of …" |
| T2 | "Make the text bigger and change the page to the night colour." | Reading settings | Text size and colour change |
| T3 | "Save this page so you can find it later." | Bookmark | Bookmark appears in the Bookmarks tab |
| T4 | "Ask the app for a summary of this page." | AI assistant | Summary appears |
| T5 | "Go back and continue reading the book from the home screen." | Continue Reading card | Opens at the saved page |
| T6 | "Add a new book by taking or choosing a photo of a printed page." | Vision scanning | Text appears and the book is saved |
| T7 | "Put your name in the app and find how many pages you have read." | Profile | Name and stats are shown |
| T8 | "Delete the book you added." | Long-press menu | The book disappears |

## 2.3 Questionnaire (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)

1. The app was easy to use.
2. Turning pages felt natural.
3. The text was comfortable to read.
4. The AI summary / word explanations were useful.
5. Scanning a paper page was useful.
6. I would use this app for studying.
7. What did you like most?
8. What was confusing or annoying?
9. What one feature would you add?
