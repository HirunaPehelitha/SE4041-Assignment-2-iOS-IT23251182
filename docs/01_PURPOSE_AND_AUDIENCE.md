## 1.1 The problem

Many readers in Sri Lanka still read mostly **printed** material: school and university notes,
photocopied chapters, library books and second-hand novels. Printed material has some real problems:

- It is heavy to carry, easy to lose, and hard to read at night without disturbing others.
- Notes and photocopies cannot be searched, and there is no easy way to remember where you stopped.
- Students reading in English as a second language often meet difficult words and long pages,
  and need help to understand them.

Popular e-book apps (Amazon Kindle, Apple Books, Google Play Books) solve some of this, but only for
books you **buy in their stores**. They cannot help with the paper material people already own.

## 1.2 The solution – BookNest

BookNest is a reading app for iPhone and iPad that turns paper into comfortable digital reading:

| Need | BookNest feature |
|---|---|
| Get printed pages into the phone | **Scan pages from photos**: Apple's Vision framework reads the text on the device |
| Read comfortably, day or night | Kindle-style reader with **white / sepia / night** pages, 4 text sizes, Dynamic Type and Dark Mode |
| Remember where you stopped | Reading position saved automatically, **Continue Reading** card, progress bars |
| Find important pages again | **Bookmarks** (tap or long-press), all bookmarks in one list |
| Understand difficult pages | **Reading Assistant**: on-device Apple Intelligence gives a summary, explains hard words, or makes a 3-question quiz |
| Stay motivated | Reading stats on the Profile screen (books started and finished, pages read) |

Everything runs **on the device**. There is no account, no internet requirement and no data leaves the phone.
This matters for students with limited mobile data, and for privacy.

## 1.3 Target audience

**Primary: university and A/L students in Sri Lanka (about 17–25 years old)**
- They read a lot of printed notes and photocopied chapters.
- Many study in English as a second language, so *Explain Words* and *Summary* help directly.
- *Quiz Me* supports exam revision.
- They usually have an iPhone or iPad but limited data, so offline features matter.

**Secondary: everyday readers and older readers**
- People who want to read their own paper books in larger text (**text size**, Dynamic Type) or at night (**night page**).
- People who don't want to buy books again just to read them on a phone.

## 1.4 How the design meets their needs (justification)

- **Simple 3-tab layout** (Library, Bookmarks, Profile): familiar from apps students already use, so no learning curve.
- **Swipe to turn pages**, like a real book and like Kindle: the most natural gesture for reading.
- **Large, adjustable serif text** and three page colours, to reduce eye strain during long study sessions.
- **On-device AI** instead of a cloud chatbot: free, private, and it works on a bus without signal.
- **Accessibility**: VoiceOver labels on covers, progress bars and buttons; Dynamic Type everywhere.

## 1.5 Similar apps and how BookNest is different

| App | Strength | What it lacks for this audience |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Kindle | Excellent reader, huge store | Only store books; no scanning of your own paper pages |
| Apple Books | Built into iOS, PDF support | No OCR of photos; no AI help on the page |
| Google Lens / Notes scanning | Good OCR | Not a reading app: no pages, progress, bookmarks or reading themes |
| **BookNest** | Scan → read → understand in one app, all offline | Smaller feature set (no store, no sync) – fine for a focused student tool |
